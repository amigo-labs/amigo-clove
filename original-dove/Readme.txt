What do I need to have installed on my computer to play DOVE?
---
40 MB free space on our hard disk for installing DOVE data and program
files, DirectX 7 (or a higher version).
And you better have a monitor, a keyboard, and a mouse.

Direct-X: you find it on driver CDs, on CDs of newer games and PC 
magazines, or - if there's really no other way - on the Microsoft 
homepage.

If you don't have the VisualBasic runtime libraries you can find and 
install them from
http://www.freeware-archiv.de, http://www.kauto.de,
or from CDs of actual PC magazines.

How does the weapon system work?
---
The gray power-ups strengthen your spaceship:

The gray round power-ups give DOVE little kind of satellites (not more
than two). They rotate around the spaceship and protect it from getting
shot.

If you want DOVE firing constantly get the symbols with the three 
turning bombs.

The square blue symbol with a circle on each corner gives you four 
power-ups, that make DOVE invulnerable - but just for a short time.

The colored extras extend your weapon system. You can improve your 
selected power-up by collecting more symbols of the same kind.
But recognize: once catching another power-up means loosing the 
power-ups you've collected before. The new power-up gives you another 
improvement but only with the standard option.

      red       |          green        |        blue
spreading laser |    reflecting balls   | concentrated twin laser 



---
Options you can select
---
By changing options you make your game play harder or easier. 

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
shooting enemies           ON  [HALF]  OFF
::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
Here you can tune the shooting speed of your enemies. 
But recognize: this does not have any effect to the shooting of a big 
boss.

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
Touching walls destroys the ship      ON  [OFF]
:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
If you turn this option off DOVE doesn't get damaged by bumping against
walls. But be careful: some enemies look like walls - the lamp in the
third level, for example.

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
Losing weapons after ship got destroyed       [ON]  OFF 
:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
If you turn this option on you will loose all power-ups when you die
in this game.

The Programm will callculate a "Point Factor" from your selection.
The points of every enemy you eliminate get multiplied with this 
factor.
For example:
A enemy you've sot gives you 100 points. The point factor is 1.25.
100 x 1,25 = 125 points!

If your point factor is higher than 1 you'll get into playing the hard 
way. Playing in this harder mode enables you to save your game, and 
return to states, you have reached before.

Can you give me some hints and advices?
---
1. Make sure to be able to navigate DOVE at any time. If you can't
   navigate using the normal arrow keys, and shoot with key 's' at the 
   same time, use the key pad for steering.

2. The completely loaded beam goes through walls. So you can hit 
   enemies even behind walls!

3. Most enemies almays go the same ways, and work from the same 
   pattern. Try to remember those, and trick your opponents!

4. Sometimes it's better to just let enemies pass rather than trying to
   destroy them.

5. Check out what weapons work most effective in the different states
   of the game.

6. Don't give up when the game seems to become to hard. Some situations
   are supposed to be to handle when you get to them the next time.
 
7. Even when the beam isn't loaded completely, it can disable the shots
   of normal enemies.
