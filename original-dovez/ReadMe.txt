1) INTRODUCTION

'DoveZ' is a newborn Arcade Shooter for your PC and revitalises this beloved genre to its best. Aiming on the maximum  of fun you can get out of this concept, 'DoveZ' deals with balanced gameplay, various scenerie and kick-ass sounds.

3) Gameplay


-Story-

There was a world, which let the sun shine. In the action of the global coalitions and all world power, the earth has been saved from alien invasion. Project DOVE was successful.
After war, the ship has been transported to zone 4. The sun broke through the clouds until the day, when the world should know, that this war is not over. 

Writing the year 2157: The science passed big impovements. But are we ready to challenge against the unknown ? The technic opened us the way to the stars. The earth is under secure by many orbital stations. 

Old legends tell us that the end of the human being is near. But we are still feeling save. During that, the work at Zone 4 in the lost Mountains have been continued. An ambitious scientist and his faithful assistent developed two outstanding, flying war machines: called D-Phyton and D-Tonator. There is only one daredevil testpilot who might control those ships.

Anyway, he has no choice. The worst case becomes true and he has to take place in one of those flying thunderbirds.

Project ‘DoveZ’ will decide whether we will stay alive – or not. 
Your task is to fight the enemy! For human being - for the earth - to win!



-Missions-

•	8 Levels (incl. Tutorial) 
•	every single level consists of 2 stages and one boss


-The Playerships-

Basically the "D-tonator" ship deals with a higher fire rate and 2 extras (to the top and to the bottom) which can be upgraded up to 4 (to the front and to the back) during the game. Those extras can be interactively turned to the front or to the back.

Each extra gets active when you collect an item like weapons or shields. (Extreme example: the D-tonator is able to use 4 different weapons simultanously which makes a lot of trouble for the enemies)

If you choose the "D-Phyton" you get a ship with a lower frequency of firing rate but stronger with single shots. 
The most special is the so called "force" which is a "plug-in" upgrade you will get always when collecting the first item on screen. It can be pulled of and on frequently and has it's own artificial intelligence. Either you may use it as a shield when plugging it to the front or the back of your ship, or as a secondary firing unit which flies on its own.



-Weapons-

Both ships have there own pool including different types of weapons (e.g. laser gun, plasma cannon, flame thrower, split fire) and several power-ups (e.g. heal, speed up / speed down, shield, bomb, homing missile)

-Beams-

The player is able to choose between 2 different kinds of beams. The first one fires a blue bundled energy stream against the enemy and gets even stronger pitching it to the limit. The second one opens a matrix, within the player is moving faster and firing stronger. That 2nd beam is called ‘Force Mode’ and needs to be fully charged to get activated. It will hold on for some seconds before you will return to your normal status.


Note: Of course you do not have all this weapons from the beginning. The player needs to collect every weapon, power up and upgrade on his own.


-Multiplayer-

It’s always good to have a friend next to you for a 2 player co-op game. Both simultanously battle with two different ships against the incoming enemies (which are even stronger to destroy). They share the amount of lifes which means if one person dies each player looses a life. It is a special challenge to play through all stages on a higher level of difficulty which moreover depends on team work. A gamepad with force feedback is recommended.


-Options-

Change the options by using the cursor keys up/down and enter.
The video resolution is 800x600 and can not be changed.
You can only use a joystick when it is connected and configured.
The controls can be changed in the options menu.

4) Technical remarks

-GENERAL HELP-
Often it helps using the newest drivers and patches for the system and hardware to avoid further graphic bugs and speed problems. Close all applications running in the background to guarantee a fluid gameplay.


---
Q: "The game doesn't start properly. An error pops up immediately.

A: You have to have a compatible graphic card with the newest drivers available. Maybe it helps starting the game with activated "voodoo" button.

---
Q: "Launching DoveZ in fullscreen-mode, the screen turns black, sounds are playing but nothing else eventually happens. What's going on?!"

A: This error occurs, using DivX6 which is an error caused by the new DivX codec. Try to
a) play the game in window-mode,
b) first deinstall DivX codecs and then install the Xvid Codec (which is included in the DoveZ install directory),
c) first install Xvid codecs and deactivate the option "Support decoding of Generic MPEG-4 video" right from the Decoder Configuration Utility tool in the DivX Codec group under "DivX" within "Programs" on your Start menu or
d) ask the DivX team for a bugfix concerning this.

---
Q: "Cutscenes don't play"

A: Install the newest drivers available for your graphic card and activate the 32-bit-colour mode when playing fullscreen. Eventually the newest version of the xVid-Codec (www.xvid.org) may help.
